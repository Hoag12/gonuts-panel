How rankings work

A hive is one Discord account with at least one linked bot that reported in the last 10 minutes. Several bots on the same Minecraft account are counted once.

The main rank orders active hives by booked profit over the last 24 hours. Equal figures share a rank.

The overall score is the equal-weighted mean of your percentile in six metrics: sales records, profit, margin and sales revenue over the last hour and last 24 hours. Only metrics reported by at least two hives are scored; a missing metric is left out, never scored as zero.

Only hives linked to this panel are ranked, and every figure is self-reported by the bot.
